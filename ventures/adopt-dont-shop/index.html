The problem

The path to adoption should be simpler than the path to a breeder.

100,000+ pets enter UK rescues every year, but adoption is slow and fragmented. Buying from a breeder is easier than adopting from a rescue.

Rescues lack modern tools

Most run on spreadsheets, email and Facebook posts. Listings, applications, home checks and outcomes all live in different places -or no place at all.

Adopters bounce between sites

Charity websites have inconsistent UX. The under-40 segment expects Tinder-quality discovery, not a PDF form.

The solution

A consumer app plus rescue SaaS that owns the full journey.

For adopters

Swipe-based discovery with smart matching on lifestyle, home and experience. One profile, applications across multiple rescues, clear status tracking from application to home check to adoption.

For rescues

A dashboard for listings, applications, home checks and outcomes. Built-in messaging and document handling. Analytics on time-to-adoption and applicant quality.

Market

Pet ownership is digital-native for the under-40 segment.

Go-to-market

Cluster, then influence, then national.

Two-sided marketplaces don't survive a national cold start. We onboard density first.

Phase 1 · Cluster

Onboard 20 rescues across London and Manchester. Density before reach.

Phase 2 · Influence

TikTok and Instagram pet creators drive adopter installs alongside organic.

Phase 3 · National

Rescue dashboard rollout UK-wide. Vet, insurer and food-brand partnerships layer on revenue and supply.

Where we are

Alpha -in active build, pre-outreach.

No public waitlist, no rescue outreach yet. Engineering is the gate; we open up to rescues and adopters once the beta build clears its quality bar.

Consumer app & rescue dashboard

Both applications in active development on the studio's TypeScript monorepo -three React frontends, a Node/Express API, full Docker dev and prod environment.

Brand & design system live

Identity, voice and component library are settled. Future feature work plugs into the system rather than re-inventing it.

Beta target: Q4 2026

The first version we'll open to a closed cluster of UK rescues. Public launch follows once the beta cohort proves out adoption flow.

What's shipped already inside the alpha build is documented on the studio journal.
