Who else sees your data

Our service providers, and nobody else.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visit requests on our behalf. Emails are stored with our email provider. We may also share data if the law requires it.

Cloudflare runs a global network, so a request may be handled outside the UK. Cloudflare’s data processing terms cover those transfers.

Links to other sites

Other sites have their own notices.

This site links to GitHub and to our ventures’ own sites. When you follow a link, that site’s privacy notice applies. AdoptDontShop will publish its own privacy notice before its beta starts.

Your rights

You can ask us about your data at any time.

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or give it to you in a portable format. Email hello@ideasquared.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy, tell us first.

Email hello@ideasquared.co.uk with “Complaint” in the subject line. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

If you’re still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We update this notice when the site changes.

If we add analytics, a form or a newsletter, this notice changes first and the date at the top changes with it. The site’s code is public, so you can check what it does on GitHub.
