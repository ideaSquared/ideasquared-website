The thesis

Most software gets rebuilt for no good reason.

Auth. Deploys. Observability. Billing. Every new project drags its team back through the same plumbing. We think a small studio, a shared platform and a bias toward shipping can compound across a portfolio. Each project the studio releases makes the next one cheaper to build.
