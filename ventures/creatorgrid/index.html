The problem

Creators post everywhere. Their personal site goes stale the moment they publish it.

A YouTube channel, a TikTok profile, an Instagram grid — each lives on a different platform, under that platform’s rules, next to every competitor. A personal site brings it together, but staying on top of it is a full-time job nobody has time for.

Your content is scattered

Followers on YouTube don’t know about your TikTok. Instagram fans miss your long-form videos. Every platform is a silo, and there’s no single place a fan can see the full picture.

Personal sites go out of date immediately

Building a portfolio site takes real time, and keeping it current takes even more. Most creators’ personal sites show work that’s months old — because updating manually doesn’t scale.

What it is

Connect your accounts once. Your site updates itself.

For creators

Sign up, connect your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, and get a public page with your content laid out in a clean masonry grid. Add a bio and your links. Done. Every time you post somewhere, it shows up on your page automatically.

What’s free

The free tier includes a fully functional public creator page with automatic content sync. Free pages include a small number of CreatorGrid promotional tiles in the content grid. Paid plans (coming soon) remove the tiles, speed up syncing, and unlock further customisation.

What’s live at alpha launch

What you can do today.

YouTube, TikTok & Instagram

Connect any combination of the three. Posts import on a schedule — videos, shorts, reels and thumbnails all included. Disconnect any platform at any time from your dashboard.

Your public page

A clean, fast-loading public page at a CreatorGrid URL. Posts display in a true masonry grid that adapts to each piece of content — no awkward gaps or forced aspect ratios.

Profile customisation

Add a bio, set your profile links and control how your content is displayed. Your page reflects your identity, not a generic template.

Account security

Two-factor authentication is available from day one — enable it in your account settings to require a one-time code at every sign-in. Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are published and readable before you sign up.

Business model

Free to start. Paid tiers for those who want more.

CreatorGrid runs on a freemium model. The free tier is permanently free — paid plans remove ads, increase sync frequency and unlock advanced features as they ship.
