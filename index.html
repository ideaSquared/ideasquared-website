The lifecycle

From bold idea to defensible business.

Every venture moves through a four-stage lifecycle, each with a defined exit criterion. Kill criteria are mandatory.

01 · IDEA

Problem hypothesis

Market sketch and a kill-or-keep call within weeks. We start with a painful, paid-for problem — not a technology in search of one.

02 · PROPOSAL

Validate demand

Willingness-to-pay and regulatory feasibility are tested before any production code. Mandatory kill criteria are set here.

03 · PROJECT

Build on the platform

Shared product, engineering, design, legal and ops construct the MVP. Design partners onboarded; first paying users.

04 · PRODUCT

Spin out & scale

Independent legal entity, follow-on capital, growth. i² retains 30–50% and stays useful as a partner, not a parent.
