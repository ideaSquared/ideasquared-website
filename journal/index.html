AdoptDontShop 12 Aug 2026

Hidden and archived pet listings are now fully invisible across the whole platform.

A gap in the “similar animals” feature on listing pages meant that a hidden or archived pet could sometimes be detected — even though it wouldn’t appear in search results or open directly. Hidden listings are now treated consistently everywhere: browsing or searching gives no indication that a hidden animal exists.

AdoptDontShop 12 Aug 2026

Admin: conversation history now loads older messages correctly.

In the admin moderation panel, clicking “Load More” in a conversation would re-load the most recent messages instead of fetching the older ones. Older messages now load in correctly, and the button disappears once you’ve reached the beginning of the conversation.

AdoptDontShop 10 Aug 2026

Deleting your account now asks you to confirm your password first.

When you request to close your AdoptDontShop account, you now have to enter your current password before the deletion goes through. This deliberate confirmation step means a borrowed device or an open browser tab can’t trigger account deletion without your knowledge.

AdoptDontShop 10 Aug 2026

Security: tightened protection for one-time sign-in links.

We closed the remaining ways that single-use codes — the kind inside password-reset and email-verification emails — could be captured by our analytics. An earlier fix caught the most common path; this one closes two further edge cases. These codes are now stripped before any analytics data is recorded, regardless of where in the address they appear.

AdoptDontShop 10 Aug 2026

Rescue and admin dashboards: multi-page selections and date sorting now work correctly.

Using “select all” on a table and then paging forward was silently clearing the selection, making bulk actions across large result sets impossible. Selections now hold across pages. Date columns were also sorting alphabetically rather than by date — they now sort in the correct chronological order.

AdoptDontShop 10 Aug 2026

Security: error messages no longer include internal platform details.

Some error responses were including more information about how the platform works under the hood than intended. Error messages now reveal only what’s needed to explain what went wrong — nothing more.

Operating principles · Journal

How this page is kept honest.

Within 24 hours

Every shipped feature gets a changelog line within twenty-four hours of going live.

Customer-facing language

Written for the people who use the product, not the engineers who built it. No commit-message jargon.

Workflow-breaking only

Bugs noteworthy enough to break a workflow get listed. Trivial fixes don’t clutter the page.
