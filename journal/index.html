WARDOGS 23 Oct 2026

End a player’s 10-kill streak and earn a VIP whitelist slot for the next day.

A new reward is now active by default: if you stop a player who is on a streak of 10 or more kills, you earn a whitelist slot valid until midnight the following day. Your account page shows that your VIP was earned this way and notes the streak you broke. All wins are kept until the slot expires and paid out together.

WARDOGS 23 Oct 2026

The bot now lists the server’s regular players by name in its public stats.

The lifetime stats endpoint now names the server’s current regulars — players who have been active on at least 14 of the past 30 days. Players who have set their profile to private are counted in the headline total but not listed by name.

GAMINGINIT 23 Oct 2026

The Lifetime page now names the server’s current regulars — players active on at least 14 of the past 30 days.

A new section on the /lifetime page lists the players who currently qualify as regulars, each with a link to their player page. Players with private profiles are counted in the headline figure but not shown by name. The regular count shown on the page follows the bot’s live number.

GAMINGINIT 23 Oct 2026

Your account page now says when your VIP was earned by breaking a kill streak.

If your VIP whitelist slot was earned by ending another player’s kill streak, your account page now says so — noting the streak length and crediting it as the source of your VIP status.

GAMINGINIT 21 Oct 2026

A new Lifetime page shows the server’s stats since launch — players, return rates, maps and peak hours in one place.

The website now has a dedicated Lifetime section in the Stats menu covering the server’s full history: daily and weekly player counts, how many new arrivals returned in the following weeks, the maps that have been played most, the times of day when sessions typically end, and a full hour-by-hour heatmap. A 7-day rolling average smooths out individual quiet days. All of the data can be downloaded as a CSV.

WARDOGS 21 Oct 2026

The bot now keeps a permanent day-by-day record — and the public stats reflect the all-time picture accurately.

The bot now stores a day-by-day log of matches and player activity, kept for good. The public stats draw on this record to report long-term figures: typical daily player counts, regular-player retention, and peak-hour patterns. The record handles edge cases correctly — overnight sessions, first-day visits, removed matches, the 30-session threshold for “regular” status, and lapsed regulars needing a new run of activity to count again. The busy-times display now only counts hours from sessions where the server actually came out of seeding; quiet attempts where nobody joined are excluded from the averages.

WARDOGS 20 Oct 2026

After every match, the bot now recognises milestones, personal bests, and rivalries — and crowns a Player of the Month.

Players who didn’t finish in the top positions now receive a private in-game message after each match highlighting something personal: a career milestone (100th match, 1,000th kill, 100 hours played), a personal best, how close they are to breaking into the website’s top 10, or a note about their nemesis — the player who has taken the most of their kills. Seeders already in-game get a thank-you when the server goes live. Each month the player with the most MVP awards is named Player of the Month in Discord, and a weekly challenge rotates to the top performer. Players who’ve linked their account and been away for a while may receive a personal Discord message inviting them back when the server seeds.

WARDOGS 20 Oct 2026

In-game messages now say your name, and leaderboard players get a personalised welcome back.

The in-game welcome and end-of-match shoutouts now address each player by name. If you’re ranked on the website leaderboard, your welcome-back message tells you your current best position. Players who top the scoreboard at the end of a match get their own MVP shoutout. Staff can edit all of these in the Lines tab on the website.

WARDOGS 20 Oct 2026

Server operators can now offer VIP for a single seed day, valid for three days.

The bot now supports two VIP models. The original: seed on several days in a week and earn a week of VIP. The new option: seed on one day and earn a 3-day whitelist slot, renewed automatically if you seed again before it expires. In-game seeding messages, Discord posts and personal messages reflect whichever model is active.

WARDOGS 20 Oct 2026

In-game messages are now shorter and end with one direct link.

Every in-game broadcast has been trimmed to lead with what matters — how many players to go live, the match result, your position — and ends with a single link you can type. Mid-match messages now send players to the vote page or leaderboard rather than a vague invite. The seeding message now advertises free VIP and links the seeding page directly.

WARDOGS 20 Oct 2026

Two more weapons are now identified: the M14 rifle and the Molotov.

Weapon ID WEPN_034 is now named the M14, and the Molotov has been added to the weapon list. Both now appear correctly in kill feeds, player stats, and the staff headshot-analysis and weapon-rules tools. Past kills with these weapons have been updated automatically.

GAMINGINIT 19 Oct 2026

The busy-times chart now shows weekday and weekend patterns separately.

The server’s busiest-hours chart previously merged weekday and weekend traffic into a single average. It now shows each separately, so you can see at a glance when the server is busiest on a typical weekday versus a typical weekend day.

GAMINGINIT 18 Oct 2026

GAMINGINIT 18 Oct 2026
