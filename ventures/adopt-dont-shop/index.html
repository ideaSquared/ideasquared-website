The problem we’re testing

Small rescues lose volunteer time to admin.

This is our working hypothesis, and the beta is how we test it. We haven’t proved it yet.

For rescues

Listings are spread across several sites. Applications arrive by email, phone and paper form. Small, volunteer-run rescues spend time they don’t have keeping it all in step.

For adopters

Every rescue has a different process. There is no single place to find rescue animals, apply, and see where an application stands.

What it is

Listings and applications, together, in one place.

For rescues

List an animal once. Receive structured applications, where every adopter answers the questions you choose. Track each application through to a decision. The rescue keeps the final say on every adoption.

For adopters

Search rescue animals in one place. Apply through one consistent form. See where your application stands.

Where we are · November 2026

Built, and waiting for its first rescues.

The adoption flow works end to end. There are no live users and no revenue yet. Every update since September is in the changelog below, newest first.

What’s built

Three web apps on one platform: the adopter site, the rescue dashboard and an admin tool. Automated tests run on every change.

What’s next

An 8-week closed beta with 3 to 5 rescues in Cheshire and Greater Manchester, planned for early 2027. We’ll set each rescue up in person.

What we don’t know yet

Whether rescues will use it unprompted, and whether adopters will apply through it. The beta numbers answer both.

The gate

The numbers that decide what happens next.

We wrote these down before the beta starts. They are targets, not results. A completed adoption is a stretch goal, because adoptions often take longer than 8 weeks.

AdoptDontShop · closed beta gateWritten down 8 Nov 2026
Go, if all four are met
  • 3+rescues active, logging in every week
  • 20+animals listed in total
  • 10+adoption applications through the platform
  • 2+rescues say they would keep using it
Stop

Fewer than 2 rescues active, or fewer than 5 applications, by week 8.

Anything in between

Fix the main problem and extend by 4 weeks. Once.

For rescues

Built for how rescues actually work.

One dashboard for the whole journey — listings, applicants, home checks and outcomes — replacing the spreadsheets, inboxes and Facebook posts you juggle today.

Listings & discovery

Publish a pet once and appear in swipe-based, cross-rescue discovery. Role-based staff access keeps the right people on the right listings.

Applications & home checks

Applications, home checks and outcomes tracked in one place — with your own application questions and auto-saving drafts for applicants.

Messaging & documents

Real-time chat with unread counts and search, plus secure document and image handling behind expiring private links.

Events

Create and publish events, register and check in attendees, and report on turnout — without leaving the dashboard.

Analytics & reports

See time-to-adoption and applicant quality at a glance, with reports you can save and schedule.

Your own pages

A simple content editor for your rescue’s pages and announcements — no developer required.

Trust & safety

Your data, and your applicants’, handled properly.

Rescues handle sensitive personal data. The platform is built so that’s safe by default, not an afterthought.

Data protection built in

Adopters can download a copy of their data or ask for it to be deleted. A data protection impact assessment is being completed before the beta starts.

Strict rescue isolation

Hard separation between rescues — one rescue can never see another’s applicants or attendee data.

Account security

Two-factor authentication with single-use recovery codes, plus protection against sign-in and invitation abuse.

Audited & reviewed

A tamper-evident audit trail with sensitive data hidden, a safety check before every release, and every high-priority security finding resolved.

What it costs

The core is free for rescues. That’s a written decision.

Listings and applications stay free. Most rescues run on donations and volunteers, and a monthly fee would stop the ones who need it most. If there is ever a paid part, it will be optional extras. AdoptDontShop is free for adopters.
