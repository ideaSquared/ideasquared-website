Pairflix

Pairflix helps two people agree on what to watch. Say what mood you’re in and how long you’ve got, and it suggests one title from the services you already have.

The problem

Streaming made content abundant. It also made deciding harder.

Streaming services recommend for one viewer at a time. When two people sit down together, the choosing can take longer than it should. That’s the hunch. We haven’t tested it with real households yet.

How it works

A relationship-first recommendation layer.

Pairflix sits on top of the streaming subscriptions a household already pays for and recommends a single, agreed title — based on both partners' tastes, mood and the time they've actually got.

Pair profile

Two — or more — taste profiles joined into one shared recommender. The whole household, not just one viewer.

Mood & time inputs

"Thirty minutes, something funny" returns one match, not two hundred. The decision, not the catalogue.

Cross-platform

Works across Netflix, Prime, Disney+ and friends. One match, wherever it actually streams.

Watch-together history

Tracks what you've actually agreed on — not just what you started — and learns the household's real taste over time.

Where we are · September 2026

Early alpha — pre-launch, no public users yet.

The platform stack is in place and September's build completed production readiness: security hardening across sign-in, two-factor verification, and password reset (rate limiting, immediate session revocation for suspended accounts, timing-safe verification); Privacy Policy and Terms of Service accessible inside the app; a clear session-expiry notice in place of an error; the billing pipeline wired; and a CI deploy target. The product hasn't opened to outside testers — no waitlist, no launch date. We'll announce when the build clears its quality bar.

Three apps in build

Client (browse, watchlists, tonight picker with provider launch buttons), Admin (user management, content moderation) and a Hono API on Cloudflare — all in the same TypeScript monorepo on the studio platform.

Platform readiness

TMDB integration for titles and streaming availability across films and TV series. Household pick tracking records every accepted, swapped or dismissed recommendation, and builds a shared taste profile over time — refined by genre, era, tone and how long you tend to watch. Provider badges deep-link directly to Netflix, Prime, Disney+ and friends. Recommendations sharpen with every rating. A public landing page lets anyone try a real pick without signing up.

Next gate

Pairflix has no proposal yet. It needs go and stop numbers before it gets more than occasional evenings, and that waits for the AdoptDontShop decision.
