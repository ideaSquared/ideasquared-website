The problem

Streaming made content abundant. It also made deciding miserable.

High

Reported "what to watch" friction

Decision fatigue, abandoned sessions and low-grade household friction are the steady cost of choice paralysis.

How it works

A relationship-first recommendation layer.

Pairflix sits on top of the streaming subscriptions a household already pays for and recommends a single, agreed title — based on both partners' tastes, mood and the time they've actually got.

Pair profile

Two — or more — taste profiles joined into one shared recommender. The whole household, not just one viewer.

Mood & time inputs

"Thirty minutes, something funny" returns one match, not two hundred. The decision, not the catalogue.

Cross-platform

Works across Netflix, Prime, Disney+ and friends. One match, wherever it actually streams.

Watch-together history

Tracks what you've actually agreed on — not just what you started — and learns the household's real taste over time.

Where we are

Early alpha — pre-launch, no public users yet.

The platform stack is in place; the product hasn't opened to outside testers. No waitlist, no beta cohort, no public release date. We'll announce when the build clears its quality bar.

Three apps in build

Client (browse, watchlists, tonight picker with provider launch buttons), Admin (user management, content moderation) and a Hono API on Cloudflare — all in the same TypeScript monorepo on the studio platform.

Platform readiness

TMDB integration for titles and streaming availability. Household pick tracking records every accepted, swapped or dismissed recommendation, and builds a shared taste profile over time. Provider badges deep-link directly to Netflix, Prime, Disney+ and friends. Recommendations sharpen with every rating.

Next gate

Closed alpha with a small invited cohort once the matcher and partner-pairing UX are stable. The aspirational targets below describe the post-launch trajectory we're building toward.

Who’s this for?

How Pairflix works, what it costs, and when you can use it.

For households

Free to end the standoff. Premium when you want more.

Pairflix sits on top of the subscriptions you already pay for — it doesn’t replace them. The core is free; premium is for the households that want the full cross-platform picture.

Free

Your pair profile, the mood & time picker, and daily matches across your services. Ad-light — enough to settle most “what shall we watch?” standoffs in about thirty seconds.

Premium

Unlimited matches, full cross-platform coverage and a richer watch-together history that keeps learning your household’s taste — a small monthly fee per household.
