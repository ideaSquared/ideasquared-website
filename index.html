How we work

From bold idea to defensible build.

01

Pitch & shape

You bring the insight. We pressure-test the idea and shape a venture brief together — usually inside a fortnight.

02

Build on the platform

We reuse the studio's TypeScript scaffolding — auth, deploys, observability — so you skip the plumbing and ship to real users in weeks.

03

Launch & learn

Real users, real feedback, real defensibility. Every project ships open source so the next one starts further down the road.

04

Spin out or fold in

Some projects graduate into standalone products. Others fold their lessons back into the platform. Both are wins.

"Build the thing. Ship the thing. Open-source the thing. The next idea starts where the last one left off."
