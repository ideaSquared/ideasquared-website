The problem

The path to adoption should be simpler than the path to a breeder.

100,000+ pets enter UK rescues every year, but adoption is slow and fragmented. Buying from a breeder is easier than adopting from a rescue.

Rescues lack modern tools

Most run on spreadsheets, email and Facebook posts. Listings, applications, home checks and outcomes all live in different places — or no place at all.

Adopters bounce between sites

Charity websites have inconsistent UX. The under-40 segment expects Tinder-quality discovery, not a PDF form.

What it is

A consumer app plus rescue software that owns the full journey.

For adopters

Swipe-based discovery with smart matching on lifestyle, home and experience. One profile, applications across multiple rescues, clear status tracking from application to home check to adoption.

For rescues

A dashboard for listings, applications, home checks and outcomes. Built-in messaging and document handling. Analytics on time-to-adoption and applicant quality.

Where we are · August 2026

Alpha build is feature-complete. Closed beta is the next gate.

The core adoption flow works end to end. A month-long security-and-privacy push through July and early August is complete, and product improvements are now shipping: anyone can now browse available pets without an account, analytics exports for rescue staff, file sharing in conversations, smarter search, and a more joined-up application journey. The path to closed beta runs through signing up our first rescues.

Feature-complete alpha

The adoption journey works end to end: public browsing without an account, secure sign-in and rescue verification, listings with role-based staff access, swipe-based discovery and cross-rescue search, real-time chat with file and photo sharing, tunable notifications, secure document handling, automatic moderation, personalised matches, events, a live rescue dashboard with CSV/PDF analytics exports, and a joined-up application flow with home visit tracking and reference recording. Ten independent services under the hood, automated tests gating every change, and a July–August security hardening pass complete. Build-in-public detail lives on the studio journal.

Three apps, one platform

Three tailored apps — the adopter portal, the rescue dashboard and the internal admin tool — sit on one shared platform, built to stay quick and reliable as the number of rescues and adopters grows.

Demand signal preserved

Letters of intent from five rescues and a waitlist of over 1,000 adopters carried over from the previous cycle. Local-first: we turn those letters into signed rescue agreements at closed beta, not before.

What we cut from scope

A rescue ratings system (against principle), a “wishlist” feature (animal-as-listing — no thanks), and premium rescue placements (the £40k cheque we turned down). Build-in-public notes live on the studio journal.

Who’s this for?

The software, data protection and pricing for UK rescues.

For rescues

Built for how rescues actually work.

One dashboard for the whole journey — listings, applicants, home checks and outcomes — replacing the spreadsheets, inboxes and Facebook posts you juggle today.

Listings & discovery

Publish a pet once and appear in swipe-based, cross-rescue discovery. Role-based staff access keeps the right people on the right listings.

Applications & home checks

Applications, home checks and outcomes tracked in one place — with your own application questions and auto-saving drafts for applicants.

Messaging & documents

Real-time chat with unread counts and search, plus secure document and image handling behind expiring private links.

Events

Create and publish events, register and check in attendees, and report on turnout — without leaving the dashboard.

Analytics & reports

See time-to-adoption and applicant quality at a glance, with reports you can save and schedule.

Your own pages

A simple content editor for your rescue’s pages and announcements — no developer required.

Trust & safety

Your data, and your applicants’, handled properly.

Rescues handle sensitive personal data. The platform is built so that’s safe by default, not an afterthought.

GDPR built in

One-click data deletion, published records of processing, and a signed-off data-protection impact assessment.

Strict rescue isolation

Hard separation between rescues — one rescue can never see another’s applicants or attendee data.

Account security

Two-factor authentication with single-use recovery codes, plus protection against sign-in and invitation abuse.

Audited & reviewed

A tamper-evident audit trail with sensitive data hidden, a safety check before every release, and every high-priority security finding resolved.

Pricing

A subscription scaled to your size — and free for adopters.

A tiered monthly subscription based on how big your rescue is. Free for adopters keeps your applicant pipeline full, and partner revenue from vets, insurers and food brands helps keep your price low. Full tiers are published at closed beta.

Go-to-market

Local first, then influencers, then national.

A marketplace with rescues on one side and adopters on the other can’t launch everywhere at once. We build up a strong local base first.

Phase 1 · Local

Turn the letters of intent from five rescues into signed agreements and bring 50–100 invited adopters from the existing waitlist into a single-region closed beta. Depth before breadth.

Phase 2 · Influence

Pet creators on TikTok and Instagram bring in adopters alongside word of mouth. Public launch opens the rescue dashboard beyond the first invited group.

Phase 3 · National

20 active rescues and the first 100 adoptions by early 2027, then a UK-wide rollout. Partnerships with vets, insurers and food brands add both revenue and supply.
