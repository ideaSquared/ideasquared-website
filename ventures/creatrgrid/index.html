The problem

Creators post everywhere. Their personal site goes stale the moment they publish it.

A YouTube channel, a TikTok profile, an Instagram grid — each lives on a different platform, under that platform’s rules, next to every competitor. A personal site brings it together, but staying on top of it is a full-time job nobody has time for.

Your content is scattered

Followers on YouTube don’t know about your TikTok. Instagram fans miss your long-form videos. Every platform is a silo, and there’s no single place a fan can see the full picture.

Personal sites go out of date immediately

Building a portfolio site takes real time, and keeping it current takes even more. Most creators’ personal sites show work that’s months old — because updating manually doesn’t scale.

What it is

Connect your accounts once. Your site updates itself.

For creators

Sign up, connect your YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch accounts, and get a public page with your content laid out in a clean masonry grid. Add a bio and your links. Done. Every time you post somewhere, it shows up on your page automatically.

What’s free

The free tier includes a fully functional public creator page with automatic content sync. Free pages include a small number of CreatrGrid promotional tiles in the content grid. Paid plans remove the tiles, speed up syncing, and unlock further customisation — sign up to upgrade.

What’s live at alpha launch

What you can do today.

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram & Twitch

Connect any combination of the four. Posts import on a schedule — videos, shorts, reels, broadcasts and thumbnails all included. Content thumbnails are stored on CreatrGrid’s own servers and served directly — they load reliably and won’t break if a platform changes its CDN. Disconnect any platform at any time from your dashboard. Twitch also shows a live banner on your public page while you’re streaming, and lets you choose which content types to import (broadcasts, highlights, clips).

Your public page

A clean, fast-loading public page at a CreatrGrid URL, served from the network edge for fast repeat visits. Posts display in a true masonry grid that adapts to each piece of content — no awkward gaps or forced aspect ratios. Channels with a lot of content load in batches with a “Load more” button. When someone shares your page link on social media, the preview shows your name, bio, and profile picture.

Profile customisation

Add a bio, set your profile links and control how your content is displayed. Your page reflects your identity, not a generic template.

Account security

Email verification is required before you can sign in. Changing your email address requires confirmation via a link sent to the new inbox — your old address receives a notice at the same time. Two-factor authentication is available from your account settings. Repeated failed sign-in attempts trigger a temporary lockout. Sign-in sessions log the IP address and device — visible in your account so you can spot logins you don’t recognise. Your 2FA secrets are encrypted in the database. Session tokens are stored in a one-way hashed form — a copy of the database cannot be used to take over an account. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited per IP address as well as per account. Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are published and readable before you sign up.

Custom domain (paid plans)

Point your own domain name at your CreatrGrid page. A single DNS record is all that’s needed — SSL is provisioned automatically, no extra certificates or manual configuration required. Available on Pro plans.

Built-in help centre

Step-by-step guides are available directly inside the dashboard. The first article covers connecting a custom domain, written for creators with no DNS or technical background.

Business model

Free to start. Paid tiers for those who want more.

CreatrGrid runs on a freemium model. The free tier is permanently free — paid plans remove ads, increase sync frequency and unlock advanced features as they ship.
